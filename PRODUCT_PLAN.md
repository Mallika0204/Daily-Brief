# Daily Brief — Product Plan

## 1. Product Vision

Build a global news and intelligence platform covering India and the world across business, politics, economics, technology, science, brands, industries, sports, entertainment, society and other important domains.

Users should be able to discover news in written and video formats, follow developments and understand their broader significance.

## 2. Target Users

- People who want a convenient way to follow current affairs.
- Students preparing for MBA classes, group discussions and interviews.
- Professionals interested in companies, industries, markets and global developments.
- Marketing and growth professionals following brand strategy, consumer trends and competition.

## 3. Core Product Requirements

1. Broad Indian and international news coverage.
2. Topic, country, company and industry discovery.
3. Written stories with publication dates, source names and original links.
4. Eligible live news streams, recorded reports and video explainers.
5. Clear separation between reported facts and analytical interpretations.
6. Business and marketing analysis for selected relevant stories.

## 4. Proposed Differentiation

Daily Brief should go beyond aggregating headlines by helping users understand connections between developments.

Potential capabilities include:

- Explaining implications for companies, customers and competitors.
- Connecting stories to industry trends and management concepts.
- Comparing strategic alternatives and their trade-offs.
- Providing evidence-based discussion questions and interview takeaways.
- Identifying recurring business signals when the available data supports meaningful analysis.

These are product hypotheses to validate, not completed features.

## 5. Initial MVP

The first working version should prioritise:

- A responsive website with a clear news feed.
- A manageable set of reliable news sources covering multiple domains and regions.
- Working written-news links and source attribution.
- Topic filters and basic search.
- A video section using legitimate, embeddable content.
- Structured analysis for selected stories.
- Basic error handling, testing and documentation.

Live streams will be included where suitable sources permit embedding. Comprehensive global coverage and continuous real-time updates will be developed progressively.

## 6. Trust and Content Principles

- Preserve original source links and publication information.
- Respect copyright, licensing and publisher restrictions.
- Avoid presenting AI-generated claims as verified facts.
- Identify uncertainty and distinguish evidence from interpretation.
- Never fabricate statistics, consumer reactions or business outcomes.

## 7. Technical and Cost Principles

- Prefer free or affordable tools where practical.
- Verify API quotas, update delays and usage rights before integration.
- Keep secrets and API keys out of public source code.
- Use understandable, maintainable code.
- Test functionality before describing it as complete.

## 8. Success Criteria

The project will be evaluated using measurable criteria such as:

- Accuracy of article links and displayed publication dates.
- Reliability of news retrieval.
- Duplicate-story frequency.
- Availability of video embeds.
- Quality and traceability of selected-story analysis.
- User feedback on usefulness and time saved.

Metrics will be reported only after they have been measured.

## 9. Current Status

Product discovery and feasibility research are in progress.

The technology stack, final feature scope, data sources and success thresholds remain to be finalised.
