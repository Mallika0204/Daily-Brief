# Daily Brief

**A global news and business intelligence platform.**

Daily Brief is an independent project designed to bring news and current affairs from India and around the world into one accessible platform. It aims to help users discover important developments across industries, companies, brands, technology, economics, politics, science, sports and society.

## Vision

Go beyond headlines by combining accessible news discovery with video content, written reporting, reliable source attribution and evidence-aware analysis.

## Planned Features

- **Global News Coverage:** News from India and international sources across multiple domains.
- **Written News:** Headlines, publication dates, source information, article links and concise summaries.
- **Video News:** Available live news streams, recorded reports, interviews and explainers from legitimate sources.
- **Topic Discovery:** Explore news by country, topic, industry, company and brand.
- **Business Intelligence:** Understand the potential implications of selected developments for companies, consumers, competitors and industries.
- **Marketing & Growth Insights:** Explore brand positioning, pricing, campaigns, product launches, consumer behaviour and competitive strategy.
- **MBA Discussion Mode:** Connect selected stories to relevant management concepts, strategic trade-offs and discussion questions.
- **Source Transparency:** Distinguish reported facts from analysis and clearly identify uncertainty where relevant.

## Development Approach

Daily Brief is being developed from scratch as an AI-assisted coding project. The focus is on learning by building, validating real functionality, documenting decisions and keeping the implementation understandable.

## Current Status

**Stage:** Initial setup and product discovery.

The features listed above are planned, not yet implemented or tested.

## Guiding Principles

- Prioritise credible sources and traceable information.
- Respect publishers' content, licensing and embedding restrictions.
- Avoid presenting unsupported AI-generated interpretations as facts.
- Prefer practical, measurable functionality over unnecessary complexity.
- Document limitations and report results honestly.

## Project Goals

Build a useful, responsive and deployable product that demonstrates product thinking, business analysis, responsible use of AI and practical technical learning.
